# Surface: home (Folha 01)

Status: approved on stated assumptions (the author is absent; the README section and the existing folder answered every question), 2026-10-29.

**Who:** a parent on a phone deciding whether Saturday is worth 26 euros; a teacher at a desk checking that a class visit is serious; a child looking over a shoulder. **Device:** phone first (390), desktop for the teacher (1440).

**Job:** `visit` (`anatomy.md`). The action is today's hours and the address, in the hero beside the ticket button; the lead picture is what is on now (the exhibits, as numbered plates); getting there sits with the hours; the page ends on visit information and the address, with the ticket action once more. No mailing list.

**Content and flows, in page order:**

1. Nav: wordmark, four section links, the PT/EN switch, the ticket button. The nav carries the job's own word, "Comprar bilhetes".
2. Hero: sheet number, the three-word head "Toca. Testa. Descobre.", one sentence, the ticket button, two facts (opening time, address). Fig. 1: the pendulum diagram, numbered callouts, the formula, swinging at its real period; the bob can be dragged and released; a pause control under the figure.
3. Experiências: three of the forty exhibits as numbered plates (N.º 07, 12, 21) with a duotone photograph, a line-art inset, a wall label (what to do, what to see), age and time. The cards tip on a fulcrum on hover.
4. Como é a visita: the four steps of a visit as a real `<ol>` with times; they reveal in order with a drawn tick.
5. Para escolas: the dark ink chapter; weekday mornings, the teacher's guide, three terms (per pupil, teachers free, group size), the email action.
6. Preços e horário: the ticket stub (three prices, the ticket button, a sample booking ref) beside the hours table, last entry, address and how to get there. This is the close.
7. Footer: wordmark, address, hours, phone slot, the sample-page line.

**Density:** marketing page, one idea per screen; the plates and the steps are the two objects with mass; running text at 34 to 52ch.

**Samples the author replaces:** hours, prices, the address, the booking reference, the school terms, the demonstration times, the EN strings. The phone number is a fill slot (`patterns.md`, "A fact the author did not give"); the rest stay as the folder had them and the footer says the page is an example.

**How it carries the identity:** the manual grammar in every object: sheet and figure numbers in the data face, callout circles, hatched beam, dimension lines; the one yellow on the button, the bob and the ticks; the spring on presses and the lever on cards; ink that never overshoots (plate wipe, diagram pen); the schools chapter switches the ground to ink so the page has two papers. Reduced motion: every state change instant, the pendulum hangs at rest, drag still works, the pause control hides.

**Chrome decision:** the nav is wordmark, links, language, action because a museum visitor needs the ticket from every screen and the language switch is a real control; the footer repeats only the facts a visitor leaves with (address, hours, phone) and says the page is a sample.

**Waivers on the record** (`gates.md` §2): `three-tiles` (the three entries are the catalogue's numbered rooms, each a plate with its own data); `numbered-markers` not needed (the steps are a real `<ol>`); the drawn pendulum and insets are the identity's device, not a stand-in for a photograph (judged gate, answered in `verification.md`).
