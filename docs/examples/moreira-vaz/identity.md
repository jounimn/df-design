# Identity: Moreira & Vaz, the pocket rights handbook

`df-design: path=design mode=design scope=home (guide, intake, 404 ledgered, not started) watermark=none load=fast selling=now tier=fast — "12-lawyer employment-law firm in Lisbon, workers not companies; homepage, a your-rights-at-work guide, intake form, 404; the current site looks like every other law firm (navy, gavel, handshakes); a site I find clear: gov.uk" — proceeding on stated assumptions: the author is absent, every answer below is read from README.md "Moreira & Vaz" and the folder as it stood on 2026-10-29`

Conditions:    Scope: homepage first, then the rights guide, the intake form and the 404, one at a time. Constraint: escape the law-firm default (navy, gavel, stock handshakes) and do not dress the firm as the state. Reference given: gov.uk, "a site I find clear". Path and mode read from the README: design / design. Watermark not asked: none. Playwright allowed (default). Stock images: yes, for the "Quem somos" band only (README, existing assets.md). Loading tier: fast, as the README records the author's answer. Selling-positioning: now, at the fast tier (brief default: presentation tier = site tier).
Intent:        "We represent workers, not companies." The site must say, before anything else, whose side the firm is on, and then be useful to someone who was just dismissed, not paid, harassed or moved to another shift: tell them what the law gives them, how long they have, and how to start. Plain language, the way gov.uk is plain. Copy in European Portuguese: "Defendemos trabalhadores. Nunca empresas."
Practical:     Language pt-PT only (an EN version is on the Later list, not asked for). Locale: Portugal; dates dd/mm; references to the Código do Trabalho (CT) by article. Flow: a visitor reads the situation index or the guide, then sends their case through the intake form or calls; the firm answers in two working days; the first consultation is priced before it is booked (README copy). The form posts nowhere yet: no endpoint exists, so it validates and says so (`patterns.md`, "A form that posts nowhere yet"). Users and devices: first-time visitors, many on a phone, often in distress; reading at arm's length. Facts the brief did not give: the phone number and the street address (two slots on the page, `patterns.md` "A fact the author did not give"); the Ordem dos Advogados registration number (omitted until given). Every legal deadline on the page is a sample and is labelled as one; the author checks each against the Labour Code before real use (README).
Grammar:       Type: Source Serif 4 (opsz) for display and the index entries, 64px hero at -0.02em, section heads 40 to 44px; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text at 19px / 1.55, measure 60 to 66ch; IBM Plex Mono only for deadlines and article numbers (instrument role: real data). Colour: paper #F4F5F0 and white bands, ink #1D2420 (green-biased), muted #55605A, rule #C8CEC7, one accent bottle green #0B6E4F (hover #08533B), error #B3261E kept separate from the accent. Geometry: 4px radius on controls, 0 elsewhere; 2px ink rules open a table or index, 1px rules divide rows; a 15rem margin column carries the article references like a handbook's marginalia. Surface: flat paper, no shadows, no gradients; the one photograph is a static CSS duotone in ink on paper. Motion: none (design mode); the only transition is a 150ms arrow shift on index hover, off under reduced motion.
Origin:        gov.uk, classed LAYOUT and TYPOGRAPHY (README). Taken: plain language, task-first headings ("O que lhe aconteceu?"), the form pattern (error summary at the top of the form, inline errors, labels above fields, hints as a line). Left: the look, because a firm dressed as the state could be mistaken for an official body. Library entry: Field guide / almanac (`identities/print-craft.md`), read specifically as a worker's pocket rights handbook: entries indexed by situation, dense but ruled, article references in the margin. Seen at (library): almanac.com tables, audubon.org/field-guide entry structure. Rejected in the README's shortlist: institutional gravitas (sides with institutions), Constructivism (reads as 1974 party politics in Portugal), Risograph (cheer feels trivial to someone just dismissed).
Never:         Navy, a gavel, columns, a handshake. The state's look (gov.uk black bar, its yellow focus, its crown-and-sans). A card grid; three circled icons; an eyebrow over every heading; tracked caps labels; a mono face on anything that is not a deadline or an article; a success message from a form that sent nothing; a figure not given by the brief; a stock photograph of a lawyer; cream-serif-terracotta.
Icons:         Phosphor Icons, Regular weight (MIT), inline SVG symbol sprite, `fill="currentColor"`, 1em and `aria-hidden` beside a text label. Regular weight matches the 19px Atkinson text (medium weight, 1.5 to 2px optical stroke). Used only where a control or status needs an affordance: phone, arrow in the index, warning-circle (form errors), check (valid field), arrow-square-out (Livro de Reclamações), map-pin (address), book-open (the guide). No icon in a feature list.
Images:        Author said yes for the "Quem somos" band only. One image: Pexels 2566380 (Magda Ehlers, calçada at the Rossio, Lisbon), Pexels License, no credit required, sized variants from the Pexels CDN, no watermark at 100%; treatment: static CSS duotone in ink on paper, 3:2 crop. Record: `assets.md`. Every other band is type, a table or a form by design.
Loading tier:  fast, the author's answer in the README ("fast (~120 KB). Medium and High: nothing this identity needs"). Three Google Fonts families (subset by Google), one AVIF with JPEG fallback, about 1 KB of plain JavaScript for the form. Works without JavaScript: every band is visible with scripting off and the form degrades to a plain post that goes nowhere.
Surfaces:      home (this run). Guide ("Os seus direitos no trabalho"), intake form page, 404: in scope for the project, ledgered below, not started; nothing was built for them. Out of scope: nothing else named.
