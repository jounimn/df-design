The year in photographs

Every picture the museum's photo desk kept this year, turned like a globe and then hung in a drum you can walk into. Scroll to turn them.

As a globe

Twenty-four frames on a sphere. The ones facing you are lit; the ones turning away go dark.

As a drum

The same frames hung in a ring. Keep scrolling and you walk in, until they stand round you.
