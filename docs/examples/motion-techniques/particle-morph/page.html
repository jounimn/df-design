One cloud, four shapes

A test page for the particle-morph card. The same points form a shell, a ring, a double helix and a disc as the page scrolls; the words below carry the story without the drawing.

Shell

Every point sits on a sphere: the whole set, at rest.

Ring

The shell flattens and opens into a ring that turns slowly.

Helix

Two strands wind upwards: the ring is pulled apart and twisted.

Disc

The strands spill outwards into a flat disc with a bright core.
