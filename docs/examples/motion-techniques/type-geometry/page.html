Knots that hold

A Saturday class in the rope loft: the bowline, the reef, the sheet bend and the clove hitch, tied until the hands know them. Their names run round the rope as it turns.

A test page for the type on geometry card. The names are drawn once into a strip of texture and the ribbon carries it; the words slide along the rope while the knot turns. The class itself is the text of this page, not the rope.
