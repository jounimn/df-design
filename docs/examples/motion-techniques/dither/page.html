Fog in the channel, lifting later

The night forecast for the coast, read at twenty to one. The fog drifts across the page in dots of one ink; carry the lamp over it, or leave it and the lamp sweeps on its own.

A test page for the Medium dither card. The fog is computed at one cell per seven pixels into a small buffer, thresholded through a 4 x 4 ordered dither and drawn up with hard pixels. Without the canvas the page is its paper.
