Intent:        "I want it to feel like an old neighbourhood newsletter." A small sourdough bakery in Porto that tells the street what tomorrow's bake is and lets people put their name on a loaf before it is baked. The reference's one lesson, "show the product working", is kept: the first screen is tomorrow's loaves, how many are left and when orders close, not a bread photograph.
Practical:     Language pt-PT only (an English version is on the Later list, nothing on the page promises one); currency EUR written `3,80 €`; times written `18h00`. Flow: pre-order tomorrow's bread by 18h00, pick up from 7h30 the next day, pay at the counter on collection, never online; the broa bakes only on Tuesdays and Thursdays; counts per loaf are limited and shown. Payment methods accepted at the counter and a telephone number are not in the brief: both are missing-fact slots on the page. Users: neighbours and regulars on a phone in the evening, a few on a desktop; the admin ("today's orders" on a counter tablet) and a 404 are named in the brief and out of this run's scope. Placeholder facts kept from the README page and marked as samples: sheet number 214, the date, the counts (12 of 40, 7), the prices, order number 0412, Rua de Cedofeita.
Grammar:       Type: Routed Gothic (lettering-guide caps, monoline) for the nameplate, headline, band heads, stamps and step numerals; Courier Prime for everything typed (body, lists, prices, form); no third face; the stamp is the only tracked, uppercase small type and the lettering caps run at 1.04 leading. Colour: duplicator paper #E5E8D5 as the ground, a raised slip #F5F5EC for the day's sheet and the bread band, ink #22211C, second impression #4A4B40, one violet stamp #4B2E83 used only where a fact changes state (remaining counts, ESGOTADA, PAGO); no other hue. Geometry: 2 to 3px radii, typed rules (hyphens and equals signs struck on the machine) instead of CSS hairlines, dotted leaders between a loaf and its price, a 3px offset shadow under the slip and nothing else lifted. Surface: flat paper; photographs greyscale, multiplied onto the paper with an ink layer in `lighten`, one duotone for the set, 4:5 crops. Motion: none; the page is static (design mode); the only transitions are interaction states, collapsed under reduced motion.
