02Concept and positioningA proposal

See every request, end to end.

For the backend engineers and SREs who are asked where the time went, Tracewell is the open-source tracer that follows one request through every service it touches and draws the answer as a waterfall. It runs on their own infrastructure, speaks OpenTelemetry, and keeps no feature behind a key.

Concept: where the milliseconds went. Every surface shows a request crossing services, and one span that took too long.

03Audience, archetype, voiceA proposal

Audience

  • Backend engineers and SREs choosing a tracing tool, at work on a laptop with a second screen.
  • Platform teams who must keep span data inside their own network.
  • Readers arriving from a GitHub README, often on a phone, who decide in one screen.

Archetype

The Sage

Knowledge through evidence. Tracewell shows the trace instead of claiming speed; the waterfall is the argument, and the slow span is found, not guessed.

Voice

  • payments.charge took 96 ms, 2.1× its p99.
  • One line to instrument.
  • There is nothing on this page to buy.
  • Supercharge your observability.

The waterfall is the argument.

04The mark20-unit grid
root span, 18 units child, 10 units, starts at 4 grandchild, 6

The mark is a trace waterfall. Three spans, each starting inside its parent and ending before it does. The orange child is the slow one: the span the product exists to find.

  • bar2.5 units high, 1 unit corner, the site's 2px span bar at scale
  • pitch5.75 units from bar to bar, so the three read as rows
  • offsets1, 5 and 9 units: each child is nested, never centred
  • colourink, signal orange, the third ink; never two orange bars
