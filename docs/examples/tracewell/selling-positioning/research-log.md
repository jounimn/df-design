# Research log: Tracewell (selling-positioning)

Access date for every entry: 2026-10-29. Each case was opened in headless Chromium (`.research/sheet.mjs`, one fresh
browser context per case because BP&O meters three articles per visitor and overlays the rest), captured three
times (top of the article, then scrolled about 1,400 and 3,200 px), and the captures were read on contact sheets
(`.research/out-cases/sheet-01.png` to `sheet-09.png`). Behance itself answers automated requests with 403 and was
not used; every case below is from BP&O (bpando.org), an open identity-case source.

What Tracewell takes from the set: open on the mark alone on the brand ground; a construction slide that explains
the drawing; one slide where the system's devices repeat; applications that are the product's own surfaces rather
than stationery; a close that repeats the action. What it leaves: the colour-burst and mascot cases, which belong to
consumer apps, and stationery-led sequences, which a developer tool does not have.
