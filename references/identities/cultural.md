# Identities: Cultural and lifestyle

Regional traditions, subcultures and lifestyle aesthetics. Source them from people inside the culture, not from a mood board; each trap names the appropriation risk.

Entry format and how to use it: `../identity-library.md`. URLs were checked live on 2026-10-05 (an answer, or a 401/403/429 bot wall on a known brand). "Awwwards entry" links are the gallery record; the live build may have been retired. Look at a site before quoting a detail from it.

---

### Afrofuturism
- **Grammar:** cosmic deep indigo and black with metallic gold, copper and iridescence. Pan-African patterns (kente, bogolan/mudcloth, adinkra) used as data-like geometry. Bold geometric or Ndebele-inspired display. Portraiture with sci-fi styling and ancestral symbols. Glowing gradients and orbiting or parallax motion, sometimes 3D.
- **Says:** Black futures, sovereignty, imagination grounded in history.
- **Fits:** Black-led culture, music, fashion, film, education, museums, speculative-tech campaigns. **Misfits:** brands without Black creative leadership, generic SaaS chasing a trend.
- **Trap:** the biggest risk is using it as a costume. Without Black authorship it is extraction. Pan-African patterns carry specific meanings (adinkra symbols, kente colourways), so learn them or leave them out. Avoid flattening Africa into one place. The Wakanda/Marvel look is a reference, not a template. Commission Black artists and credit them prominently.
- **Load:** medium to high (3D, glow, video).
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/panafrican-afrofuturism-afa-afa (AFA-AFA studio's Afrofuturist web elements: archive, gallery and music UIs); https://nmaahc.si.edu/explore/exhibitions/afrofuturism (Smithsonian NMAAHC exhibition); https://nataal.com (contemporary African culture magazine, the editorial, non-costume end).
- **Search:** Awwwards "afrofuturism"; Aesthetics Wiki "Afrofuturism"; Are.na "afrofuturism"; Behance "afrofuturist brand identity"; "adinkra symbols meaning".

### Anime official site
- **Grammar:** key-visual-first hero (full-bleed illustrated artwork). Japanese/English bilingual nav, news tickers, on-air schedule tables, character carousels. Bold gothic Japanese type with English display. Diagonal panel cuts, manga speed lines, tight info density. Trailer embeds.
- **Says:** event, fandom, IP universe, high production.
- **Fits:** anime and manga, games, light novels, character IP, Japanese entertainment. **Misfits:** western corporate, slow luxury.
- **Trap:** western imitations add anime clichés (sakura, sparkles) without the dense Japanese information design. Art rights matter, so never use fan art or generated look-alikes. Large key-visual images need responsive `srcset`.
- **Load:** medium to high.
- **Seen at:** https://chainsawman.dog (MAPPA/Shueisha franchise portal, dark key visuals, bilingual nav); https://jujutsukaisen.jp (official anime site, key-visual-led).
- **Search:** "アニメ 公式サイト デザイン"; Behance "anime website"; "Japanese anime site design gallery" (e.g. "anime web design" on Pinterest); Fonts In Use "anime"; Aesthetics Wiki "Animecore".

### Art-school indie (zine and riso)
- **Grammar:** risograph two-colour overprints (fluorescent pink plus blue), halftone and misregistration via CSS blend modes. Unusual indie typefaces (Dinamo, Velvetyne, OH no Type). Raw HTML lists, visible borders, default links, collage. Anti-grid, with browser-native elements left showing.
- **Says:** experimental, community-made, anti-corporate, curatorial.
- **Fits:** galleries, small presses, bookfairs, artist-run spaces, student shows, indie labels. **Misfits:** mainstream retail, banks, accessibility-critical public services (unless done carefully).
- **Trap:** anti-design becomes inaccessible design (unlabelled controls, low contrast, scroll-jacking). Free indie fonts often have tiny character sets, so check language support.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** https://www.printedmatter.org (artists' books nonprofit, bookfair catalogue culture); https://www.are.na (the art-school research tool, raw blocks and channels aesthetic).
- **Search:** Are.na channels "web brutalism" / "riso"; Velvetyne and OH no Type in Fonts In Use; brutalistwebsites.com; hoverstat.es; Aesthetics Wiki "Indie Sleaze" / "Art Hoe".

### Barbiecore
- **Grammar:** hot pink (#e0218a family) on everything, glitter and gloss, cursive Barbie-style script with bold rounded sans. Plastic-sheen 3D, dreamhouse set staging, sparkles and toy-box framing.
- **Says:** maximal femininity as power, camp, fun.
- **Fits:** toys, fashion capsules, events, beauty collabs, campaigns with deliberate camp. **Misfits:** neutral or serious brands, long-lived identities (it dates fast).
- **Trap:** Mattel's pink and script are trademarks, so imitate the attitude, not the marks. Pink-on-pink contrast. A trend peak (2023) that ages fast.
- **Load:** medium (3D gloss imagery).
- **Seen at:** https://shop.mattel.com/pages/barbie (the source: hot-pink brand shop).
- **Search:** Aesthetics Wiki "Barbiecore"; "hot pink website design"; Behance "barbiecore branding"; land-book "pink".

### Biophilic organic
- **Grammar:** blob and leaf-shaped image masks (`clip-path`, SVG). Moss, fern, clay and cream palette. Soft rounded humanist sans with a friendly serif. Daylight plant photography. Slow growth or unfurl motion, grain.
- **Says:** living, restorative, calm, healthy.
- **Fits:** plant retail, wellness, landscape architecture, sustainable building, food. **Misfits:** heavy industry, crypto, high-energy sport.
- **Trap:** random blob shapes are the 2020 startup template. Organic forms need a reason (an actual leaf, pebble or growth geometry). Green alone is not biophilic.
- **Load:** fast to medium.
- **Seen at:** https://www.thesill.com (plant retail, soft palette, daylight plant photography).
- **Search:** "biophilic design website"; land-book "plants"; Aesthetics Wiki "Naturecore" / "Goblincore"; Are.na "organic shapes web".

### Bohemian
- **Grammar:** earthy palette, organic arch shapes and cut frames, vintage
  landscape photography, serif plus soft script, ornament, warm grain.
- **Says:** free, natural, travelled, artisanal.
- **Fits:** travel, interiors, lifestyle, natural products, retreats. **Misfits:** technology.
- **Trap:** beige-and-terracotta default. This is one of the current
  AI-slop clusters (`recipes-design.md` §7). It needs a specific origin.
- **Load:** medium (photography).
- **Seen at:** spell.co: Byron Bay boho fashion label, earthy palette, vintage landscape photography *(own read)*; bedouinsociete.com: cited by Interior Insider as minimalist-boho.
- **Search:** Aesthetics Wiki "Boho" / "Bohemian"; trends.daisyui.com/trend/cottagecore/ and /trend/biophilic-design/ (neighbours); design-styles.enlighten-media.net/pages/organic/; Land-book "interiors" or "retreat"; Shopify "boho" store showcases.

### Brazilian tropical modernism
- **Grammar:** Burle Marx organic curves and Copan/Niemeyer sweeps as SVG section dividers. Lush botanical maximal prints (FARM) or concrete-and-greenery photography (Bo Bardi). Geometric modernist sans (Futura-likes, Brazilian foundries such as Plau) plus expressive display. Jungle green, macaw yellow and sea blue, or raw concrete grey. Wavy Copacabana-promenade stripes.
- **Says:** sun, optimism, modernist confidence, sensual nature.
- **Fits:** fashion, swimwear, architecture, music, cultural foundations, hospitality. **Misfits:** cold enterprise, minimal tech, northern-winter products.
- **Trap:** Carnival clichés and "exotic tropics" framing written from outside. Burle Marx curves used as decoration without the modernist rigour that grounds them. Source from Brazilian studios and photographers, use real place references, and write Portuguese copy natively.
- **Load:** medium (print-heavy imagery).
- **Seen at:** https://farmrio.com (Rio botanical maximal prints, colour-saturated shop); https://institutobardi.org.br (Lina Bo Bardi's Casa de Vidro, modernist institutional restraint); https://www.osklen.com (Brazilian "e-brigade" minimal-tropical fashion).
- **Search:** "Burle Marx pattern"; "Brazilian graphic design" on Behance; Plau foundry fonts in use; Awwwards filter Brazil; Are.na "brazilian modernism".

### Celtic and insular
- **Grammar:** interlace knotwork and spirals (Book of Kells) as SVG borders. Uncial or half-uncial display (sparingly) with a humanist sans. Vellum, moss, slate, deep green and gold-leaf accents. Atlantic coastline photography. Knotwork line-draw animation (stroke-dashoffset).
- **Says:** ancient continuity, myth, landscape, craft.
- **Fits:** Irish, Scottish, Welsh and Breton cultural institutions, music festivals, whiskey, heritage tourism. **Misfits:** global tech, anything without a Celtic connection.
- **Trap:** "Paddywhackery": shamrocks, leprechauns, faux-uncial everything. Some knot symbols are co-opted by hate groups (Celtic cross), so avoid them. Use the Irish and Gaelic languages properly (fadas), and work with Irish or Scottish designers.
- **Load:** fast (SVG).
- **Seen at:** https://www.visittrinity.ie/book-of-kells-experience/ (Trinity College Dublin's Book of Kells, insular manuscript as heritage brand); https://www.celticconnections.com (Glasgow folk and roots festival).
- **Search:** "Book of Kells interlace SVG"; Aesthetics Wiki "Celtic"; Fonts In Use "uncial"; Behance "Irish heritage branding".

### Chinese traditional (shan-shui and ink)
- **Grammar:** Song/Ming serif (Source Han Serif / Noto Serif SC) with a refined Latin serif. Ink-wash landscape (shan shui), seal-red (zhusha) chops, celadon, ink black and silk ivory. Hand-scroll horizontal layouts, vertical text and lattice window frames. Slow ink-bleed reveals.
- **Says:** cultivated heritage, literati refinement, continuity.
- **Fits:** museums, tea, porcelain, luxury craft, cultural festivals, calligraphy schools. **Misfits:** brands without a Chinese connection, fast fashion, edgy tech.
- **Trap:** chop-suey "wonton" Latin fonts are racist pastiche. Dragons and red lanterns are generic clichés. Mixing Japanese and Chinese motifs. Use real hanzi typesetting (simplified vs traditional chosen deliberately), subset the CJK fonts, and have native speakers check copy.
- **Load:** medium (CJK fonts, ink imagery).
- **Seen at:** https://www.shangxia.com (Shang Xia, Chinese craft-luxury house, porcelain and ink restraint); https://en.dpm.org.cn (the Palace Museum, Beijing, imperial collection site).
- **Search:** "新中式 网页设计" (new Chinese style web design); Behance "Chinese traditional web design"; "shan shui website"; Fonts In Use "Chinese"; Aesthetics Wiki "Chinese aesthetic".

### Clean-girl beauty minimal
- **Grammar:** milky pastel or nude palettes (pink, glazed-donut beige), rounded soft sans with lowercase wordmarks. Dewy skin close-ups, product bottles on pastel. Pill buttons and wide padding. Subtle hover bloom.
- **Says:** effortless, skin-first, approachable aspiration, Gen-Z wellness.
- **Fits:** skincare, cosmetics, wellness supplements, haircare. **Misfits:** heavy industry, games, anything that needs gravitas.
- **Trap:** one of the most-cloned DTC looks: millennial pink plus pill buttons. "Clean" beauty claims carry regulatory and racial-coding baggage, so show a range of skin tones. Thin pastel contrast.
- **Load:** fast to medium.
- **Seen at:** https://www.glossier.com (millennial-pink originator, lowercase friendliness); https://www.rhodeskin.com (glazed neutral palette, product-as-object minimal).
- **Search:** Aesthetics Wiki "Clean Girl"; land-book "skincare"; Godly "beauty"; Are.na "glossier".

### Club and rave flyer
- **Grammar:** big compressed or experimental display (stretched, warped, variable-axis animation). Acid green, UV, chrome or stark black-and-white. Lineup typography as the layout. Strobing hovers, marquees, 3D chrome logos, glitch, grainy party photography. Mono for timetable data.
- **Says:** energy, nightlife, community, underground credibility.
- **Fits:** festivals, clubs, promoters, DJs, labels, nightlife media. **Misfits:** daytime services, health, kids.
- **Trap:** strobing and flashing break WCAG 2.3 (seizure risk), so honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. Unreadable lineups on mobile. 3D chrome is heavy. Copying famous club identities (Berghain's ultra-plain site is a deliberate statement, not a template).
- **Load:** medium to high.
- **Seen at:** https://dekmantelfestival.com (Amsterdam festival, annual bold graphic system); https://boilerroom.tv (dark, video-led club broadcast platform); https://ra.co (Resident Advisor: listing-dense black-and-white club media).
- **Search:** Are.na "rave flyer"; Godly "music"; "techno festival identity" on Behance; Fonts In Use "festival"; Aesthetics Wiki "Rave".

### Coquette and balletcore
- **Grammar:** blush, ballet pink, ivory and cherry red. Bows, lace, pearls and ribbon motifs as small SVG ornaments. High-contrast Didone or delicate italic serif with tiny all-caps sans. Flash film photography, ruffled frames, scalloped edges. Sparkle and hover wiggles.
- **Says:** romantic, girlish, playful femininity, sometimes ironic.
- **Fits:** lingerie, dresses, accessories, beauty, bakeries, Gen-Z fashion drops. **Misfits:** anything serious or institutional, children's products (the adult connotations are real).
- **Trap:** the aesthetic carries Lolita-adjacent connotations, so keep it away from minors. Pink-on-pink contrast failures. A bow on everything becomes a meme. Pick one ornament and commit.
- **Load:** fast to medium.
- **Seen at:** https://www.loveshackfancy.com (romantic floral pink, ornate serif); https://selkiecollection.com (puff-sleeve fantasy styling, pastel romance); https://www.sandyliang.info (bows and girlish downtown, the playful-ironic end).
- **Search:** Aesthetics Wiki "Coquette" / "Balletcore"; TikTok "coquette aesthetic"; Pinterest "coquette web design"; land-book "pink fashion".

### Cottagecore
- **Grammar:** soft serif and script (Cormorant, Playfair italic, hand-lettering). Cream, sage, butter, dusty rose and gingham. Botanical watercolour or pressed-flower scans, deckled paper edges and lace dividers. Golden-hour film photography of meadows, linen and bread. Gentle drifting petals at most.
- **Says:** pastoral escape, handmade comfort, softness, seasonal living.
- **Fits:** slow fashion, bakeries, florists, farm shops, craft courses, cosy games. **Misfits:** tech, finance, urban nightlife, anything time-critical.
- **Trap:** twee overload (script body text, low-contrast pastel-on-cream). Also a romanticised rural fantasy that can erase labour and history. Real farm and maker photography beats stock prairie dresses.
- **Load:** medium (textures, photography).
- **Seen at:** https://www.shopdoen.com (Dôen: Californian vintage-pastoral fashion, film photography, soft serif).
- **Search:** Aesthetics Wiki "Cottagecore"; Pinterest "cottagecore website"; land-book "fashion vintage"; Fonts In Use "Cormorant"; Are.na "cottagecore".

### Dark academia
- **Grammar:** old-style serif throughout (EB Garamond, Caslon, IM Fell, Cormorant), small caps, drop caps, roman numerals. Espresso, oxblood, forest green, ink black and aged gold. Parchment textures, rules and fleurons, book-plate frames. Candlelit or library photography. Motion is minimal (page-turn, ink reveal).
- **Says:** erudition, melancholy, ritual, literary seriousness.
- **Fits:** publishers, bookshops, classical music, universities' literary arms, games, journals, stationery. **Misfits:** bright consumer tech, kids' learning, health.
- **Trap:** heavy parchment textures and low-contrast gold on brown make text unreadable. Faux-Latin overuse. The look romanticises elitist, white-Eurocentric institutions, so diversify the canon in imagery. The Light academia variant (cream, linen, sunlit) shares the grammar with a lighter palette.
- **Load:** medium (textures, serif families).
- **Seen at:** https://www.foliosociety.com (illustrated fine editions, serif-led, rich-toned bookish catalogue).
- **Search:** Aesthetics Wiki "Dark Academia" / "Light Academia"; Fonts In Use "Caslon book publisher"; land-book "bookstore"; Are.na "dark academia"; Behance "publishing house website".

### Desert modern and Southwest
- **Grammar:** adobe, sand, sage, sunset coral and turquoise. Wide sans or geometric display with mono details. Big-sky panoramic photography, arches and stepped mesa geometry as masks. Grain overlays, slow-scroll landscape video, sun-angle gradients.
- **Says:** spaciousness, retreat, stillness, creative pilgrimage.
- **Fits:** desert hotels, retreats, wellness, festivals, land-art institutions, ceramics. **Misfits:** urban fast commerce, dense tools.
- **Trap:** Navajo, Pueblo and other Indigenous patterns used as décor is appropriation (see Urban Outfitters "Navajo" litigation). Commission Indigenous artists or leave patterns out. Heavy landscape video stalls mobile loads.

### Gorpcore and outdoor technical

Earth tones with a hazard accent (signal orange, acid yellow). Studio product on grey, and harsh alpine photography. Precise, data-like layouts and minimal motion.
- **Says:** competence, performance, nature as technical terrain, urban-outdoor cool.
- **Fits:** outdoor gear, trail running, technical apparel, expedition travel, field-research organisations. **Misfits:** soft beauty, romantic fashion, comfort food.
- **Trap:** topo lines and coordinates as empty decoration. Spec tables nobody reads. Overlap with Technical drafting, so keep the nature photography central.
- **Load:** medium.
- **Seen at:** https://www.andwander.com (Japanese outdoor fashion, spec-precise grid and alpine photography); https://arcteryx.com (technical product storytelling, feature callouts); https://satisfyrunning.com (Paris running label, gritty trail and counter-culture photography).

### Gothic and dark romantic
- **Grammar:** blackletter or sharp Gothic display (UnifrakturMaguntia, Pirata One, custom blackletter) with clean sans UI. Black, blood red, deep purple and silver. Occult and botanical engraving illustration, candle and Victorian ornament, cathedral arches as image masks. Moonlit photography, glitter and smoke loops.
- **Says:** darkness as beauty, rebellion, mysticism, the alternative scene.
- **Fits:** alt fashion, metal and darkwave music, tattoo studios, horror publishing, occult goods. **Misfits:** healthcare, children, mainstream finance.
- **Trap:** blackletter used for body text or navigation is illegible. Blackletter also carries far-right associations in some contexts, so pair it carefully. Dark-pattern-heavy e-commerce (pop-ups, countdowns) cheapens it. Avoid sacred Christian iconography used carelessly.
- **Load:** medium.
- **Seen at:** https://www.killstar.com (occult goth fashion, black with blackletter-style display); https://www.dollskill.com (loud alt and goth retail, maximal promo layer).
- **Search:** Aesthetics Wiki "Gothic" / "Romantic Goth" / "Whimsigoth"; Fonts In Use "blackletter"; land-book "dark fashion"; Are.na "goth web".

### Hip-hop and mixtape
- **Grammar:** heavy condensed or chrome display, gold, graffiti tags and Cooper Black. Mixtape-cover collage (diamonds, cash, flames), drop-shadow chrome. Black and gold or loud primaries. Portrait photography with flash. Marquee tickers, autoplay audio (opt-in).

- **Fits:** music artists, hip-hop media, sneaker culture, urban apparel. **Misfits:** muted corporate, wellness, academic.
- **Trap:** non-Black brands borrowing hip-hop visual slang without the culture (minstrel-adjacent pastiche). Bling clip art. Credit artists and photographers, and source graffiti from real writers with permission.
- **Load:** medium.
- **Seen at:** https://www.massappeal.com (Nas-backed hip-hop media and label, bold editorial); https://jp.bape.com (A Bathing Ape, camo pattern and loud streetwear-rap heritage).

### Islamic geometric and Arabic calligraphic
- **Grammar:** girih and star-and-polygon tessellations built as SVG patterns, used as screens, masks or loaders. Arabic type (29LT Bukra or Zarid, Noto Naskh/Kufi, IBM Plex Sans Arabic) paired with a Latin partner, with full RTL mirroring via logical CSS properties. Lapis, turquoise, sand, ivory and gold. Light-through-mashrabiya shadows. Slow rotational or tessellating motion.
- **Says:** order, heritage, contemplative craft, the scholarship of Islamic art.
- **Fits:** museums, cultural centres, Gulf and MENA institutions, Arabic publishing, heritage hospitality. **Misfits:** brands with no regional connection, ironic or edgy consumer products.
- **Trap:** sacred calligraphy (Qur'anic text, the Shahada) used as decoration is offensive. Fake "Arabesque" Latin fonts are pastiche. Unshaped or left-to-right Arabic is broken: test RTL, ligatures and Arabic numerals. Hire Arabic typographers and check copy with native readers. Geometric pattern is shared heritage; calligraphy needs care.
- **Load:** fast to medium (SVG pattern, but Arabic webfonts are larger).
- **Seen at:** https://mia.org.qa (Museum of Islamic Art Doha, bilingual institutional site); https://agakhanmuseum.org (Toronto museum of Islamic arts); https://29lt.com (Arabic-Latin type foundry; the reference for bilingual typesetting).
- **Search:** "girih SVG pattern generator"; "bilingual Arabic English website" on Behance; Fonts In Use "Arabic"; Khatt Foundation; Awwwards filter UAE / Saudi Arabia.

### Japanese quiet craft (Muji / mingei)
- **Grammar:** Japanese gothic or mincho (Noto Sans JP, Hiragino Mincho, Shippori Mincho) paired with a small Latin serif or sans. Vertical text (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) used sparingly. Kinari off-white, sumi black and one muted natural accent (indigo, persimmon, moss). Generous ma (negative space), small type and object photography in soft daylight. Motion is slow opacity only.
- **Says:** restraint, provenance, seasonal attention, craftsmanship over brand noise.
- **Fits:** tea, ceramics, confectionery, ryokan, kitchenware, stationery, slow-living brands. **Misfits:** loud consumer launches, crypto, gaming, kids' entertainment.
- **Trap:** western pastiche: brush-stroke "Asian" display fonts, red suns, cherry blossom clip art, fake kanji. Authentic versions use real Japanese typesetting, a native-speaker copy check, real CJK webfont subsetting (CJK fonts are multi-MB, so use `unicode-range`) and seasonal (kigo) rather than stereotyped motifs. It also overlaps with Wabi-sabi: this entry is cleaner and retail-ready, not rough and aged.
- **Load:** medium (CJK webfonts are heavy unless subset).
- **Seen at:** https://kinto.co.jp (soft daylight product photography, airy grid, tiny type); https://www.toraya-group.co.jp (centuries-old wagashi maker, mincho type and seasonal sweets as quiet editorial); https://www.nakagawa-masashichi.jp (Nara textile house, craft-led catalogue with understated layout).
- **Search:** "japanese web design" on siteinspire / Awwwards (filter Japan); "mincho vertical writing-mode website"; Are.na "japanese web design"; Fonts In Use "Japanese"; Aesthetics Wiki "Japandi".

### K-pop comeback microsite

### Kawaii
- **Grammar:** rounded bold sans (M PLUS Rounded, Nunito, Fredoka). Pastel candy palette with one strong pink or red. Chibi mascot characters with big eyes. Stickers, sparkles, speech bubbles, rounded 20px+ corners. Bouncy spring easing, squash and stretch, emoji-like icons.

- **Load:** medium (illustration, Lottie).

### Korean soft minimal
- **Grammar:** thin Korean gothic (Pretendard, SUIT, Noto Sans KR) with a light Latin sans or a narrow serif. Bone, greige, clay and smoky-grey palettes, often monochrome per campaign. Sculptural, gallery-like product staging (stone, resin, dried plants). Text runs very small; grids are asymmetric with lots of empty field. Motion is slow crossfades and long video loops.
- **Says:** cool, gallery-adjacent, sensorial, Seoul concept-store luxury.
- **Fits:** fragrance, skincare, eyewear, concept retail, cafés, galleries. **Misfits:** mass-market value retail, B2B data products, anything information-dense.
- **Trap:** sub-11px body text and grey-on-greige fail contrast. Video-only heroes hide the product and stall LCP. Copying the Hannam-dong concept-store look without the material art direction leaves an empty beige page. Use real Hangul typesetting, with `word-break: keep-all` for Korean.
- **Load:** medium to high (art-directed video, large imagery).
- **Seen at:** https://www.tamburins.com (sculptural set design, near-monochrome, minimal UI); https://nonfiction.com (Seoul fragrance brand, soft neutral palette and quiet sans); https://www.gentlemonster.com (the surreal, installation-driven end of the same Seoul retail scene).
- **Search:** "Korean brand website" on Behance; "Seoul concept store web design"; Pretendard font in use; Are.na "korean graphic design web"; land-book "skincare minimal".

### Mediterranean summer (dolce vita)
- **Grammar:** sun-faded palette of terracotta, lemon, aperitivo red, Riviera blue and whitewash. Vintage-feeling serif or italic display (Recoleta, GT Super, Ogg, Canela) over clean sans body. Stripes (parasol, cabana), hand-drawn lemons and tiles. Film-grain lifestyle photography with sea and long lunches. Light parallax, bobbing illustrations and marquee bands.
- **Says:** leisure, warmth, conviviality, slow pleasure.
- **Fits:** aperitifs, non-alcoholic spirits, hotels, swimwear, restaurants, olive oil, travel. **Misfits:** winter goods, serious B2B, healthcare.
- **Trap:** it has become the default "summer DTC" aesthetic, with lemons and stripes on everything. Authentic versions use a specific place (Amalfi tile, Ligurian stripe, Sicilian majolica) and real regional photography rather than generic stock "Italy". Avoid cartoon mafia-trattoria clichés.
- **Load:** medium (photography, illustration, a couple of display faces).
- **Seen at:** https://drinkghia.com (Riviera palette, pattern illustration, 1960s aperitivo-poster feel); https://www.pellicanohotels.com (film-photography heritage, serif-led editorial calm).

### Mexican folk colour (Otomí, papel picado, Barragán)
- **Grammar:** saturated flat fields of rosa mexicano, cobalt, marigold and Barragán pinks and ochres. Hand-embroidered Tenango (Otomí) animals and flora as photographed textile, not redrawn clip art. Papel-picado cut-paper borders as SVG masks. Slab or condensed vernacular display (rotulación sign-painting) with clean sans body.
- **Says:** vitality, craft, heritage, celebration.
- **Fits:** Mexican-owned food and drink, artisan cooperatives, cultural institutions, fashion working with makers. **Misfits:** brands without a Mexican connection, solemn or institutional finance.
- **Trap:** high appropriation risk. Otomí designs from Tenango de Doria have been widely copied by global brands without credit or payment. Do not redraw or generate Otomí motifs. Commission artisans, name the community and makers on the page, and license the imagery. Avoid sombreros, sugar-skull Día de Muertos clichés and "fiesta" fonts. Hire Mexican designers or illustrators.
- **Load:** medium (textile photography, an SVG border system).
- **Seen at:** https://www.carlafernandez.com (Mexican fashion house working with indigenous artisans; craft documented and credited); https://www.museofridakahlo.org.mx (Casa Azul cobalt and folk palette in an institutional context); https://jarritos.com (bright Mexican-soda colour blocking at the popular end).

### Nordic print modernism (Marimekko / Josef Frank)
- **Grammar:** large flat repeat patterns (Unikko poppies, Frank botanicals) used as full-bleed section backgrounds. Bold primaries plus black on white, or saturated botanicals on linen. Clean sans UI so the pattern carries the voice. Pattern tiles are delivered as SVG or a CSS background. There is little motion beyond pattern reveals.
- **Says:** joyful, design-literate, mid-century optimism, craft through print.
- **Fits:** textiles, homeware, children's goods, cultural institutions, seasonal campaigns. **Misfits:** finance, enterprise SaaS, anything that needs neutrality.
- **Trap:** pattern overload behind text kills legibility, so keep pattern and copy in separate bands. Imitating a famous print (the Unikko poppy) reads as knock-off. Commission or license original repeats.
- **Load:** fast if patterns are SVG; medium if raster.
- **Seen at:** https://marimekko.com (signature prints as hero and product surfaces, very plain UI); https://svenskttenn.com (Josef Frank botanicals framed by a restrained serif-and-sans shop).
- **Search:** "Marimekko website"; Fonts In Use "Marimekko"; "pattern-led e-commerce" on siteinspire; Behance "Scandinavian pattern brand identity web".

### Nordic soft minimal
- **Grammar:** grotesk or neo-grotesk sans (Söhne, Neue Haas, Basis Grotesque, HAY's custom sans) set small with generous leading. Warm off-white (#f4f1ec) grounds with muted chalky accents (sage, butter, brick, powder blue). Large product photography on seamless coloured paper, square 2–4-column grids, hairline rules, no shadows. Motion is limited to slow fades and image crossfades on hover.
- **Says:** democratic good taste, calm domesticity, things built to last.
- **Fits:** furniture, homeware, lighting, design-led D2C, architecture studios, hotels. **Misfits:** urgent or high-energy offers, gaming, nightlife, anything that needs density.
- **Trap:** this is the default "tasteful e-commerce" template, so it turns generic fast. Beige plus a grotesk plus a 4-up grid gives you every Shopify theme. Without one owned colour and real product art direction it says nothing. "Hygge" copy and stock candles turn it into pastiche.
- **Load:** fast to medium (image-heavy, but CSS is trivial).
- **Seen at:** https://www.hay.com (product on coloured paper, playful colour blocking inside a strict grid); https://www.muuto.com (muted palette, quiet sans, editorial room sets); https://www.fritzhansen.com (heritage end of the same grammar, more white space and a serif-free hierarchy).

### Skate culture

### Solarpunk and low-tech web
- **Grammar:** two modes. (1) Illustrative: leafy greens, terracotta, solar yellow and sky blue, Art Nouveau curves meeting photovoltaics, rounded humanist type, optimistic community illustration. (2) Practice: dithered images, system fonts, tiny page weight, live battery or solar status indicators, static HTML. The best examples combine the two.
- **Says:** hopeful sustainability, commons, degrowth, care.
- **Fits:** climate organisations, co-ops, permaculture, civic tech, community energy, sustainable publishing. **Misfits:** luxury excess, fast fashion, crypto-maximalism.
- **Trap:** greenwashing. A 6MB hero video of a leafy city undermines the message. If you say "solarpunk", the page weight must say it too. "Put a tree on it" illustration without substance reads as stock.
- **Load:** fast (that is the point).
- **Seen at:** https://solar.lowtechmagazine.com (solar-powered server, dithered images, battery meter, the canonical example); https://compost.party (tiny site served from a phone on a solar panel).

### South Asian kitsch maximal (truck art / Bollywood)

### Tropical tiki
