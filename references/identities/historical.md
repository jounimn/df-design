# Identities: Historical movements

Art and design movements from the 1880s to the 1990s, and the broad poles of minimalism and maximalism, as they translate to the web.

Entry format and how to use it: `../identity-library.md`. URLs were checked live on 2026-10-05 (an answer, or a 401/403/429 bot wall on a known brand). "Awwwards entry" links are the gallery record; the live build may have been retired. Look at a site before quoting a detail from it.

### 70s Supergraphics / Groovy
- **Grammar:** thick parallel rainbow stripes bending round corners (Barbara Stauffacher Solomon, NASA worm era), Cooper Black, Windsor, ITC Avant Garde ligatures, Souvenir; earth palette (brown, burnt orange, harvest gold, avocado) or saturated rainbow. Rounded corners, wavy bands, sunsets. Bouncy, elastic motion.
- **Says:** warmth, nostalgia, easy-going optimism.
- **Fits:** food & drink, consumer goods, music, skate/surf, hospitality, cannabis. **Misfits:** enterprise, legal, high luxury.
- **Trap:** default "retro" template look (Cooper Black + sunset stripes); low-contrast earth tones; overused since 2020 DTC wave.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/la-revoltosa-rebooting-a-70-year-old-spanish-soda-brand-for-the-scroll-generation.html (La Revoltosa — redrawn 1970s logo for scroll site); https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/kalso-mega-scrolling-storytelling-experience (Kalso — 70s shoe brand storytelling); print: NASA 1975 Graphics Standards Manual reissue https://standardsmanual.com.
- **Search:** Awwwards inspiration_search "70s"; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Category:1970s; Fonts In Use "Cooper Black"; "Barbara Stauffacher Solomon supergraphics"; "ITC Avant Garde ligatures web".

### Art Brut / Outsider art
- **Grammar:** untrained, obsessive mark-making: biro hatching, dense repeated patterns, handwritten text crawling round images, crayon and pencil on found paper; uneven hand-drawn type, raw paper scans. Naïve colour or monochrome. Minimal motion; hand-drawn hover scribbles.
- **Says:** authenticity, raw creativity, the unpolished human.
- **Fits:** galleries, art therapy, children's charities, independent musicians, craft. **Misfits:** corporate, luxury, tech.
- **Trap:** patronising imitation of marginalised artists; scanned handwriting as body text; overlaps Conceptual sketch — Art Brut is obsessive and naïve, not designerly.
- **Load:** medium (scans).
- **Seen at:** https://www.artbrut.ch (Collection de l'Art Brut, Lausanne — Dubuffet's founding collection); https://www.rawvision.com (Raw Vision — outsider-art magazine).
- **Search:** "outsider art graphic design"; "Collection de l'Art Brut"; "Raw Vision magazine"; "naive hand-drawn website"; aesthetics.fandom.com search "Outsider Art".

### Art Deco
- **Grammar:** geometric display caps (Broadway, Parisian, Poiret One, Limelight, Josefin Sans, Futura Display); tall narrow letterforms, wide tracking. Black/ink-navy/emerald grounds with gold/brass foil, cream. Symmetry, stepped ziggurats, sunbursts, fans, chevrons, thin double rules framing panels. Matte-plus-metallic surfaces. Slow, centred reveals; line-draw SVG borders.
- **Says:** glamour, ceremony, old money, Jazz Age confidence.
- **Fits:** hotels, cocktail bars, spirits, theatre, jewellery, galas, luxury residential. **Misfits:** SaaS dashboards, healthcare, children's products, anything needing dense data.
- **Trap:** Gatsby-party pastiche; gold text on black fails contrast at body sizes; all-caps display faces used for paragraphs; metallic gradients rendered as flat mustard on screens.
- **Load:** fast (SVG ornament, one display + one text face); medium if foil textures are raster.
- **Seen at:** https://videinfra.com/work/poklonnaya-9 (Poklonnaya 9 — Awwwards SOTD residential site built on its Deco facade, stepped frames, gold-on-dark); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/varani-gin (Varani Gin — 1920s/Gatsby gin journey, Deco–Nouveau ornament; Awwwards entry); https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/art-deco-hero-section-doof-media (Art Deco hero section by antidefault).
- **Search:** fontsinuse.com/tags/2431/art-deco; trends.daisyui.com/trend/art-deco/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Art_Deco; "art deco website" Awwwards; "art deco hotel website design".

### Art Nouveau
- **Grammar:** whiplash curves, botanical ornament, framed panels with arched tops; organic display faces (Arnold Böcklin, Auriol, Eckmann, Cloister; modern: Fraunces soft, Recoleta) with a calm serif text. Sage, ochre, rose, muted teal, aged ivory; gold linework. Mucha-style halo roundels, mosaic. Slow vine-growth SVG stroke animation.
- **Says:** craft, nature, romance, fin-de-siècle refinement.
- **Fits:** perfume, tea, botanicals, wine, florists, museums, fantasy publishing. **Misfits:** tech, fintech, sport, utilitarian tools.
- **Trap:** clip-art frames from stock; illegible display faces as headings; ornament crowds content on mobile; heavy raster borders.
- **Load:** fast with SVG ornament; medium with scanned lithographs.
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lart-nouveau (L'Art nouveau — Gobelins/Musée d'Orsay forest walk through Guimard's work; Awwwards entry); https://www.mucha.cz (Mucha Foundation — Mucha artworks as page identity); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/varani-gin (Nouveau ornament mixed with Deco).
- **Search:** trends.daisyui.com/trend/art-nouveau/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Art_Nouveau; Fonts In Use "Arnold Böcklin" / "Eckmann"; "Mucha inspired website"; "art nouveau web design".

### Arts & Crafts
- **Grammar:** William Morris dense botanical repeats (acanthus, strawberry thief), woodcut borders, Kelmscott blackletter-roman (Golden Type; modern: Goudy Old Style, Jenson, IM Fell). Indigo, madder red, weld yellow, olive, parchment. Hand-printed texture, drop caps, framed text blocks. Almost no motion.
- **Says:** handmade, honest materials, heritage craft, anti-industrial.
- **Fits:** furniture makers, bookbinders, heritage, textiles, independent publishers, gardens. **Misfits:** tech, fast fashion, nightlife.
- **Trap:** wallpaper pattern behind body text kills legibility; large tiled raster patterns heavy; museum-gift-shop cliché.
- **Load:** fast with SVG repeats; medium with scanned patterns.
- **Seen at:** https://www.wmgallery.org.uk (William Morris Gallery — Morris patterns throughout); https://morrisandco.sandersondesigngroup.com (Morris & Co. — live commerce on the original pattern archive); https://www.libertylondon.com (Liberty — Arts & Crafts/Nouveau print heritage as brand).
- **Search:** "Kelmscott Press typography"; "William Morris pattern website"; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Arts_and_Crafts; Fonts In Use "Golden Type"; "arts and crafts movement graphic design".

### Atomic Age / Googie
- **Grammar:** starbursts, atom orbits, boomerangs, parabolic arches, flying-saucer roundels; script plus casual sans (Brush Script, Atomic Age, Bullet, Futura); turquoise, flamingo pink, chrome, tomato red on cream; neon signage, roadside-sign layouts. Twinkling starbursts, neon flicker. Atompunk variant adds Vault-Boy irony, Geiger green.
- **Says:** postwar optimism, Americana, kitsch futurism (or its satirical dark side).
- **Fits:** diners, motels, bowling, games, retro events, cocktail bars. **Misfits:** serious professional services, minimal brands.
- **Trap:** Fallout imitation; neon flicker = flashing hazard; kitsch overload; script faces illegible small.
- **Load:** fast (SVG); medium with neon glow filters.
- **Seen at:** https://fallout.bethesda.net (Fallout — atompunk/Vault-Tec 1950s ad identity); Awwwards Retro collection https://www.awwwards.com/awwwards/collections/retro/.
- **Search:** aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Googie; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Atomic_Age; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Atompunk; "googie sign typography"; Fonts In Use "Atomic Age".

### Bauhaus
- **Grammar:** primary red/yellow/blue on off-white with black; circle/square/triangle as layout units; geometric sans (Futura, Universal-inspired Bayer type, Architype Bayer, DM Sans, Space Grotesk), lowercase-only headings; asymmetric grid, heavy rules, rotated vertical labels. Flat, no shadows. Shape-morph and snap-to-grid motion.
- **Says:** function, rationality, education, modernist confidence.
- **Fits:** design schools, architecture, museums, furniture, tooling, typography projects. **Misfits:** luxury, romance, wellness, heritage food.
- **Trap:** "three shapes and primaries" cliché; yellow text fails contrast; geometric shapes as decoration rather than structure.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** https://www.bauhaus-dessau.de (Bauhaus Dessau Foundation — institutional identity); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/100-years-of-bauhaus (bauhaus100 jubilee portal; Awwwards HM, 2019); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bauhaus-2-0 (Bauhaus 2.0 — one font, one colour, simple graphics; Awwwards entry).
- **Search:** trends.daisyui.com/trend/bauhaus/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Bauhaus; Awwwards "bauhaus" search; Fonts In Use "Architype Bayer"; "Herbert Bayer universal alphabet web".

### Concrete Brutalism (architectural)
- **Grammar:** the 1950s–70s béton-brut building as visual: board-marked concrete photography, monochrome grey, heavy rectilinear blocks and cantilevers as layout, stencil and municipal signage sans (Barbican's custom, DIN, Gill Sans), orange or yellow wayfinding accents. Monumental scale, slow camera-like scroll.
- **Says:** civic ambition, permanence, honesty of material, urban culture.
- **Fits:** arts centres, architecture, housing, urbanism, photography books, techno. **Misfits:** soft consumer, kids, wellness.
- **Trap:** confusion with web "Brutalism"/Neo-brutalism (raw HTML) — this is about the architecture; grey on grey contrast; heavy full-bleed photography.
- **Load:** medium (large photography).
- **Seen at:** https://www.barbican.org.uk (Barbican — arts centre in a Brutalist estate, identity built on its architecture); https://www.sosbrutalism.org (SOS Brutalism — DAM database of endangered Brutalist buildings).
- **Search:** "SOS Brutalism"; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Brutalism; "Barbican identity North"; "brutalist architecture photography website"; "béton brut graphic design".

### Constructivism / Soviet avant-garde poster
- **Grammar:** red, black, off-white (occasional yellow); hard diagonals (15–45°), wedges, cropped circles, photomontage in halftone, bold condensed grotesque (Druk, Bebas, Tusker, "Rodchenko"-style display), stepped text, loudspeaker arrows. Flat print surface, paper grain. Fast slide-in along the diagonal.
- **Says:** agitation, urgency, collective force, revolution.
- **Fits:** activism, theatre, music, festivals, political campaigns, editorial. **Misfits:** luxury, wellness, children, healthcare.
- **Trap:** Soviet iconography carries political baggage; diagonals wreck mobile reading order; red-on-black small text illegible.
- **Load:** fast; medium with large halftone photomontage.
- **Seen at:** https://www.publictheater.org (The Public Theater — Paula Scher's wood-type, constructivist-derived identity); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/bauhaus-and-vkhutemas (Bauhaus and Vkhutemas comparison site; Awwwards HM); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/ussr-futurism (USSR futurism — Soviet modernist architecture tour; Awwwards HM 2023).
- **Search:** trends.daisyui.com/trend/constructivism/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Constructivism; "Rodchenko poster typography"; Fonts In Use "Russian constructivism"; "El Lissitzky web design".

### Dada / photomontage
- **Grammar:** absurd collage: cut photos, mismatched letterpress type sizes and families (Hausmann, Schwitters Merz), stamps, typewriter, upside-down words, arbitrary arrows. Newsprint off-white, black, single red. Hard-edged paper cut-outs, no gradients. Random/irrational interactions, chance-based layout.
- **Says:** anti-art, irony, nonsense as critique.
- **Fits:** art institutions, experimental music, satire, zines, cultural anniversaries. **Misfits:** commerce, trust-critical services.
- **Trap:** randomness without wit becomes mess; overlaps with Scrapbook (warmer, personal) — Dada is cold, ironic, typographic; image-heavy collage is slow.
- **Load:** medium (many cut-out images).
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/dada-data (DADA-DATA — Akufen's interactive anti-museum for Dada's centenary, SOTD 2016; original dada-data.net now offline); https://www.cabaretvoltaire.ch (Cabaret Voltaire, Zurich — Dada's birthplace venue); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/history-of-collage (History of Collage landing page).
- **Search:** aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dadaism; trends.daisyui.com/trend/collage/; "Kurt Schwitters Merz typography"; "Hannah Höch photomontage web"; "DR.ME Cut That Out".

### De Stijl / Neoplasticism
- **Grammar:** strict orthogonal grid with thick black bars, rectangles of red/yellow/blue/white, no diagonals (Mondrian) — Van Doesburg allows 45°. Square-built lettering (Van Doesburg alphabet; modern: Neue Haas, Monument Grotesk, blocky display). CSS Grid as the artwork. Panels that resize/reflow as motion.
- **Says:** order, balance, Dutch modernism, pure structure.
- **Fits:** architecture, galleries, design studios, furniture, portfolios. **Misfits:** organic brands, storytelling, food.
- **Trap:** literal Mondrian painting as a page; colour blocks with no content role; thick bars eat mobile width.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** https://span.studio/projects/mas-context-website (MAS Context — layout drawn from De Stijl publications); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/a-tribute-to-piet-mondrian (A tribute to Piet Mondrian — WebGL/horizontal gallery; Awwwards entry); https://webflow.com/made-in-webflow/website/project-mondrian-efc396 (Project Mondrian — Webflow experiment).
- **Search:** aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/De_Stijl; speckyboy.com/de-stijl-web-design/; "Van Doesburg typography"; "mondrian css grid"; Awwwards "Mondrian".

### Emigre / Cranbrook deconstruction (postmodern type)
- **Grammar:** layered, overlapping text columns, bitmap and low-res faces (Oakland, Emperor, Matrix, Template Gothic, Keedy Sans), extreme scale contrast, deconstructed hierarchy, early-Mac aesthetics. Black/white with acid or muted accents. Layer and opacity shifts on hover.
- **Says:** theory-driven, critical, 1990s digital avant-garde.
- **Fits:** design criticism, art schools, experimental publishing, music labels. **Misfits:** commerce, onboarding flows.
- **Trap:** overlapping text fails accessibility; bitmap faces blur at fractional sizes; neighbours Grunge and Y2K — keep it typographic and theory-led.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** https://www.emigre.com (Emigre — foundry and magazine archive, original Licko/VanderLans fonts).
- **Search:** "Emigre magazine layouts"; "Cranbrook Academy typography 1990"; Fonts In Use "Template Gothic"; "Zuzana Licko bitmap fonts"; "Ed Fella lettering".

### Grunge (90s Carson-era)
- **Grammar:** distressed and degraded type (Template Gothic, Dead History, Blur, Trixie, FF Beowolf), overprinted and torn layouts, text over photos, columns running off-page, scratches, stains, ink bleed; muted dirty colour, film grain. Glitchy, jittery motion.
- **Says:** raw, emotional, alternative, intentional rule-breaking.
- **Fits:** music, skate/surf, fashion, film, magazines, youth culture. **Misfits:** finance, health, government.
- **Trap:** illegibility as default; texture overlays heavy and over-used; differs from Neo-brutalism (clean, flat) and Scrapbook (warm, personal) — Grunge is degraded, photographic, typographic.
- **Load:** medium (texture images).
- **Seen at:** https://davidcarsondesign.com (David Carson Design — the originator's own studio site); print: Ray Gun magazine (1992–95).
- **Search:** trends.daisyui.com/trend/grunge/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Grunge; "Ray Gun magazine layouts"; Fonts In Use "Dead History" / "Template Gothic"; "David Carson typography web".

### Italian Futurism
- **Grammar:** parole in libertà — words at mixed sizes, weights and angles exploding across the page; onomatopoeia; heavy slab and grotesque mix, bolted/mechanical lettering (Depero). Black, red, silver, speed-blurred machine imagery, force lines, simultaneity (repeated overlapping forms). Aggressive kinetic type motion, scroll-velocity skew.
- **Says:** speed, noise, machines, aggression, the new.
- **Fits:** motorsport, music, aviation, energy drinks, Campari-style aperitivo, experimental type. **Misfits:** calm services, healthcare, finance.
- **Trap:** movement's fascist ties; scattered type unreadable and inaccessible to screen readers if not ordered in DOM; motion sickness.
- **Load:** fast (type-driven); medium with GSAP kinetic type.
- **Seen at:** no confirmed live web build — documented print/brand: Fortunato Depero's Campari advertising 1926–36 and Campari Soda bottle (https://www.campari.com heritage); Depero Futurista "bolted book" (1927). Awwwards course "From Poster to Screen" cites "From Marinetti to David Carson".
- **Search:** "parole in libertà typography"; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Futurism; "Depero Campari graphics"; "futurist typography kinetic web"; Fonts In Use "futurism".

### Maximalism
- **Grammar:** collage density, clashing type, layered imagery, pattern on
  pattern, many focal points held by one strong grid underneath.
- **Says:** abundance, culture, personality, art.
- **Fits:** art, music, fashion, culture festivals, post-internet brands. **Misfits:** task-focused tools.
- **Trap:** no underlying grid, so it is noise rather than maximalism. The
  CTA must still win.
- **Load:** medium (image weight). Use AVIF/WebP and lazy-load below the fold.
- **Seen at:** glitche.com: photo/video glitch app, neon loading screen and layered video (Webflow's maximalism list; enlighten-media); pinupmagazine.org: layered, colliding magazine site (Webflow list); ellenporteus.com: illustrator site on the enlighten-media maximalism page.
- **Search:** design-styles.enlighten-media.net/pages/maximalism/; trends.daisyui.com/trend/maximalism/; webflow.com/blog/10-inspiring-examples-of-maximalism; awwwards.com/websites/collage/; Hoverstat.es (expressive portfolios).

### Memphis
- **Grammar:** clashing pastel plus primary (mint, salmon, lemon, cobalt, black), squiggles, confetti, terrazzo/Bacterio patterns (Sottsass), zigzags, polka dots, tilted geometric solids with hard drop-shadows; geometric or quirky display type. Flat surfaces, no gradients. Bouncy, rotating shapes.
- **Says:** irreverent, playful, anti-good-taste 80s postmodernism.
- **Fits:** design shops, toys, events, youth brands, galleries, creative studios. **Misfits:** finance, healthcare, sober corporate.
- **Trap:** "Saved by the Bell" / Memphis Lite clip-art; patterns behind text; confused with Neo-brutalism (Memphis is pattern- and shape-led, not border-and-shadow).
- **Load:** fast (SVG patterns).
- **Seen at:** https://memphis-milano.com (Memphis Milano — official archive/e-commerce site, featured on Awwwards for loader, footer, newsletter animation).
- **Search:** trends.daisyui.com/trend/memphis-design/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Memphis_Lite; "Sottsass Bacterio pattern"; Awwwards "memphis milano"; "Memphis design web pattern svg".

### Mid-century modern
- **Grammar:** warm muted palette (mustard, teal, burnt orange, olive, cream), organic boomerang and kidney shapes, flat cut-paper illustration with textured fills (Charley Harper, Mary Blair, Saul Bass), humanist/geometric sans (Futura, Neutraface, Brandon Grotesque) plus script accents. Clean modular grids, generous whitespace. Gentle offset-print grain; simple slide/hinge motion.
- **Says:** optimistic, design-literate, warm modernism.
- **Fits:** furniture, interiors, architecture tours, coffee, record shops, travel, kids' books. **Misfits:** cutting-edge tech, nightlife, enterprise.
- **Trap:** stock "retro vector" look; palette goes muddy on dark mode; grain textures heavy; Saul-Bass pastiche titles.
- **Load:** fast with SVG illustration.
- **Seen at:** https://www.modernismweek.com (Modernism Week Palm Springs — mid-century architecture festival); https://www.eamesoffice.com (Eames Office); https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/kalso-mega-scrolling-storytelling-experience (Kalso — 70s/mid-century illustrated scroll story).
- **Search:** trends.daisyui.com/trend/mid-century-modern/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Mid-Century_Modern; "Charley Harper style web illustration"; Fonts In Use "Neutraface"; "Saul Bass title sequence css".

### Minimalism
- **Grammar:** generous space, one face in two or three weights, a minimal
  palette, one focal element per view.
- **Says:** confidence, focus, premium restraint.
- **Fits:** almost anything whose product or photography is strong. **Misfits:** a subject that needs the reader to feel abundance.
- **Trap:** minimal content mistaken for minimal design. Thin pages read
  as unfinished (median measured page: 9,329px).
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** tamakiyoshida.com and mad.ac: listed on the enlighten-media minimalism page; culturedcode.com/things/: a product page with one focal element per view *(own read)*.
- **Search:** minimal.gallery (curated since 2013); design-styles.enlighten-media.net/pages/minimalism/; trends.daisyui.com/trend/minimalism/; siteinspire "Minimal" style; Land-book "minimal".

### Op Art
- **Grammar:** black-and-white (or two vibrating complements) stripes, concentric rings, warped checkerboards, moiré; geometric sans kept tiny and neutral. Pattern is the hero; depth illusions from line-width modulation. Scroll- or cursor-driven warping (SVG/canvas/shader).
- **Says:** perception, sixties modernity, hypnotic precision.
- **Fits:** opticians, galleries, fashion, music, events, hypnotic product launches. **Misfits:** text-heavy, accessibility-critical, health.
- **Trap:** photosensitive-epilepsy risk (WCAG 2.3) with moving high-contrast stripes; moiré on resampled raster; text over pattern unreadable; must respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Load:** fast (SVG/CSS repeating-gradient); high if shader-driven.
- **Seen at:** no confirmed live web build — documented brand/print: Lance Wyman's Mexico 68 Olympics identity (https://www.lancewyman.com); Vasarely Foundation (https://www.fondationvasarely.org); Bridget Riley's 1960s stripe paintings.
- **Search:** "op art css repeating-gradient"; "moiré shader codrops"; "Mexico 68 Lance Wyman identity"; aesthetics.fandom.com search "Op Art"; "Vasarely pattern web".

### Polish poster school
- **Grammar:** painterly, conceptual single image with visual metaphor; hand-lettered type integrated into illustration; brushy gouache texture, collage (Cieślewicz), surreal anatomy (Starowieyski); bold flat colour with rough edges. One big image per screen; slow crossfade.
- **Says:** intellectual wit, cultural seriousness, artistic authorship.
- **Fits:** film festivals, theatre, opera, books, cultural institutions. **Misfits:** e-commerce catalogues, SaaS, data products.
- **Trap:** needs real illustrators — AI/stock pastiche shows; hand lettering as images harms accessibility and SEO; one-image layouts thin on content.
- **Load:** medium (large painted rasters).
- **Seen at:** https://www.polishposter.com (Polish Poster Gallery — archive/shop of the school's posters); print: Tomaszewski, Lenica, Świerzy film posters (1950s–80s).
- **Search:** "Polish School of Posters"; "Henryk Tomaszewski poster lettering"; "Roman Cieślewicz collage"; Fonts In Use "Polish poster"; "Polski Projekt posters typefaces".

### Pop Art
- **Grammar:** Ben-Day halftone dots, thick black outlines, flat CMYK primaries plus hot pink, speech bubbles, action bursts, repeated silkscreen grids (Warhol), comic display type (Bangers, Komika; or Futura Bold condensed). Flat, printed surface, misregistration offset. Snappy pop/scale motion.
- **Says:** fun, mass culture, irony, consumer bravado.
- **Fits:** snacks, drinks, entertainment, retail, events, comics. **Misfits:** luxury, finance, serious civic.
- **Trap:** Lichtenstein-pastiche cliché; halftone raster bloat; comic fonts look childish for adult brands; yellow-on-white contrast.
- **Load:** fast (CSS radial-gradient halftone); medium with silkscreen photo grids.
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/warhol-arts (Warhol Arts — SOTD Mar 2025, Warhol-themed); https://www.warhol.org (The Andy Warhol Museum); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/pop-site (Pop Site by Turbine Studio; Awwwards HM 2025).
- **Search:** trends.daisyui.com/trend/pop-art/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pop_Art; "CSS halftone Ana Tudor"; Awwwards "pop art"; Fonts In Use "pop art".

### Psychedelic 60s
- **Grammar:** melting, warped lettering filling shapes (Wes Wilson, Victor Moscoso; faces: Hippie, Funky, Bluu Next fluid display, Canobis), vibrating complementary colour pairs (orange/blue, magenta/green), kaleidoscope, paisley, liquid light-show blobs. Fluid SVG/shader distortion, hue cycling.
- **Says:** altered state, counterculture, joy, music festival.
- **Fits:** gigs, festivals, records, cannabis/psychedelic therapy, craft beer, fashion drops. **Misfits:** B2B, healthcare (outside psychedelic therapy), finance.
- **Trap:** unreadable lettering for anything but the headline; vibrating colour pairs fail contrast and fatigue; liquid shaders heavy on mobile; flashing violates WCAG 2.3.
- **Load:** medium; high with WebGL liquid.
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/locus-solus (Locus Solus — "interactive tour in the 60s", SOTD 2016; Awwwards entry); https://www.awwwards.com/favsto/collections/trippy-sites/ (Trippy Sites collection — Swiss Army Man, Adult Swim Singles); https://www.awwwards.com/haus-creative-agency-case-study.html (HAUS — psychedelic kaleidoscopic sphere redesign).

### Punk xerox / ransom note
- **Grammar:** photocopy grain, crushed blacks, ransom-note letters cut from magazines, typewriter and marker scrawl, safety pins, tape, stencil; black on xerox white with fluoro pink/yellow (Jamie Reid). Diagonal pasted fragments. Jump cuts, stutter animations.
- **Says:** DIY, anger, independence, anti-establishment.
- **Fits:** bands, gigs, activism, streetwear, independent zines, skate. **Misfits:** luxury, institutions, finance.
- **Trap:** brands cosplaying rebellion; ransom letters as images break text search and screen readers; heavy scanned textures.
- **Load:** medium (scanned textures, many images).
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/punk-me-1 (Punk Me — web documentary on punk 1976–today; Awwwards HM 2014); print: Jamie Reid's Sex Pistols artwork (1976–77).

### Space Age / 60s retro-futurism
- **Grammar:** white, silver, orange and space-black; rounded "pod" shapes, capsule cards, circular windows; extended or rounded futurist sans (Eurostile, Microgramma, Countdown, Space Mono for data); NASA-era mission graphics, planet gradients. Slow orbital motion, countdown sequences.
- **Says:** exploration, optimism about technology, the future as it was imagined.
- **Fits:** aerospace, science museums, audio hardware, fashion, sci-fi media, launches. **Misfits:** heritage craft, organic food, legal.
- **Trap:** overlaps Synthwave and Cyberpunk if neon creeps in — keep it white/orange daylight; Eurostile everywhere reads as cheap sci-fi; 3D planets heavy.
- **Load:** fast; high with WebGL planets.
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/retronova (Retronova — retrofuturist fashion, SOTD Sep 2024); https://www.awwwards.com/sites/nasa-spacecrafts (NASA Spacecrafts WebGL, SOTD); https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/galleries/visions-of-the-future (JPL Visions of the Future — retro travel posters).
- **Search:** aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Space_Age; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Retrofuturism; Fonts In Use "Eurostile"; "NASA graphics standards manual"; Awwwards "retro futuristic".

### Streamline Moderne
- **Grammar:** late-Deco stripped of ornament. Rounded or geometric sans (Kabel, Metro, Futura, Bank Gothic; avoid high-contrast Didones), italics for speed. Cream, chrome-silver, ocean-liner navy, signal red. Triple horizontal speed lines, pill shapes, rounded leading edges, porthole circles, long horizontal bands. Horizontal slide/parallax motion.
- **Says:** speed, optimism, machine-age travel.
- **Fits:** rail/travel, automotive, diners, appliances, retro tech, hospitality. **Misfits:** editorial long-form, finance, organic/natural brands.
- **Trap:** reads as generic "retro diner"; horizontal-band layouts break on narrow mobile; chrome gradients look cheap without restraint.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** no strong live site found — documented print: Henry Dreyfuss's 20th Century Limited (1938) and 1933–34 Chicago World's Fair graphics; web demo at https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/streamline-moderne/.
- **Search:** trends.daisyui.com/trend/streamline-moderne/; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Streamline_Moderne; "streamline moderne graphic design"; Fonts In Use "Kabel" or "Bank Gothic"; "1930s travel poster speed lines".

### Surrealism
- **Grammar:** impossible photo composites (objects floating, scale
  violated), a dreamlike sky, clean type that lets the image do the work.
- **Says:** imagination, a twist, brand as idea.
- **Fits:** creative agencies, travel, fashion campaigns, launches. **Misfits:** operational pages.
- **Trap:** one striking image and nothing after it; the composite quality must be high.
- **Load:** medium (large hero imagery).
- **Seen at:** weak, currency unconfirmed. toggl.com: cited (iDevie) for surreal 3D duck composites; redmonkeys.de: cited for a hybrid vehicle-plane hero. Check both before quoting.
- **Search:** creativemarket.com/blog/design-trend-report-surrealism; Adobe Creative Trends 2025 "surrealism"; awwwards.com/websites/surreal/; Godly or Land-book "3D" plus "campaign"; Behance "surreal campaign website".

### Swiss design
- **Grammar:** a strict modular grid, flush-left grotesk, asymmetric
  balance, large type as composition, red/black/white, objective photography.
- **Says:** clarity, rigour, nothing superfluous.
- **Fits:** design studios, architecture, institutions, B2B tools with something to state. **Misfits:** whimsy.
- **Trap:** "Helvetica plus a grid" without the asymmetric tension reads as a template.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** swissted.com: punk flyers redrawn as International Typographic Style posters; grillitype.com: Swiss foundry site built on a hard grotesk grid *(own read)*; swissdesign.systems: listed on the enlighten-media Swiss page.
- **Search:** design-styles.enlighten-media.net/pages/swiss/; trends.daisyui.com/trend/swiss-style/; awwwards.com/websites/swiss/; Fonts In Use "Neue Haas Grotesk" or "Akzidenz-Grotesk" web; siteinspire "Swiss" style tag.

### Swiss Punk / New Wave typography
- **Grammar:** Swiss grid deliberately broken: letterspaced and stepped type, mixed weights within a line, typographic layers, screen-dot and film-overlap textures, rules and boxes as composition (Weingart, Odermatt & Tissi, April Greiman early digital). Akzidenz/Helvetica as base, distorted. Colour often black + one hot colour; Greiman adds pixel and video. Layer-shift and parallax motion.
- **Says:** educated rebellion, typographic experimentation within rigour.
- **Fits:** design schools, type foundries, architecture, cultural programmes, music. **Misfits:** mainstream retail, healthcare, accessibility-first services.
- **Trap:** reads as an unfinished layout; collides with the existing "Swiss design" entry if the grid is not visibly broken; letterspaced body text harms reading.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** https://madeinspace.la (Made in Space — April Greiman's studio site); print: Weingart's Kunstgewerbemuseum Basel posters (1970s–80s).
- **Search:** "Wolfgang Weingart poster"; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/New_Wave; Fonts In Use "Weingart"; "April Greiman digital typography"; "swiss punk typography website".

### Ukiyo-e / Japanese woodblock
- **Grammar:** flat colour areas with key-block black outline, bokashi gradients (Prussian blue to white), stylised waves and clouds, cartouche title boxes, vertical text; washi paper grain. Indigo, vermilion, ochre, sage. Brush or Mincho faces with a Latin serif. Parallax layered planes like print blocks; slow wave loops.
- **Says:** Japanese craft, narrative tradition, calm drama.
- **Fits:** Japanese food, sake, tea, travel, games set in Japan, museums, tattoo. **Misfits:** brands without Japanese connection (appropriation risk), fintech.
- **Trap:** Great Wave cliché; cultural appropriation; raster prints heavy; vertical text breaks on mobile without writing-mode care.
- **Load:** medium (illustration rasters); fast if vectorised.
- **Seen at:** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/ukiyo (UKIYO — Awwwards HM Jul 2023); https://ukiyoeheroes.com (Ukiyo-e Heroes — woodblock prints of game characters, e-commerce); https://ukiyo-e.org (Ukiyo-e.org — 220k+ print search archive for references).
- **Search:** aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Ukiyo-e; Awwwards "Japan" websites; "bokashi gradient css"; "Hokusai inspired web design"; "japanese woodblock illustration website".

### Victorian
- **Grammar:** ornate scripts and didones, gilded frames, oil-painting
  imagery, deep reds, greens and gold, symmetrical centred composition,
  flourishes.
- **Says:** heritage, theatre, opulence, the antique.
- **Fits:** typography and art courses, museums, tea, theatre, heritage brands. **Misfits:** modern utilities.
- **Trap:** costume. A contemporary layout carrying Victorian *objects*
  (a framed painting, a script wordmark) beats wall-to-wall ornament.
- **Load:** medium (imagery, a script webfont).
- **Seen at:** penhaligons.com: perfumer to Queen Victoria; the Born Group redesign aims at "elegant classicism with a touch of humour and eccentricity"; hendricksgin.com: Victorian-curiosity gin brand (enlighten-media lists it under Art Deco; Victorian is *(own read)*).

### Vienna Secession / Wiener Werkstätte
- **Grammar:** rectilinear Nouveau: square grids, chequerboards, gold leaf, black-and-white squares, strict framed panels, lettering with geometric quirk (Koloman Moser; modern: Secession-derived "Wiener Melange", Otto Wagner revivals). Black/white/gold, occasional teal. Grid of small squares as border. Minimal motion.
- **Says:** cultured, architectural, Viennese rigour with ornament.
- **Fits:** galleries, architecture firms, coffee houses, design shops, classical music. **Misfits:** mass consumer, gaming, youth.
- **Trap:** collapses into Klimt-gold kitsch; chequer borders vibrate on high-DPI screens; geometric display faces illegible small.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** https://www.secession.at (Secession, Vienna — the building's own institution site; restrained, the identity lives in its lettering and gold-leaf heritage); https://www.leopoldmuseum.org (Leopold Museum — houses Klimt/Schiele/Werkstätte holdings). Print reference: Ver Sacrum magazine (1898–1903).
- **Search:** "Ver Sacrum typography"; "Koloman Moser graphic design"; aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Vienna_Secession; Fonts In Use "Secession"; "Wiener Werkstätte pattern".
