# Identities: Industry archetypes

How each sector actually presents itself in production, what its generic default looks like, and how the best examples escape it.

Entry format and how to use it: `../identity-library.md`. URLs were checked live on 2026-10-05 (an answer, or a 401/403/429 bot wall on a known brand). "Awwwards entry" links are the gallery record; the live build may have been retired. Look at a site before quoting a detail from it.

---

### Academic / research lab
- **Grammar:** serif body (Charter, Source Serif, Tiempos), wide margins with sidenotes, figures as first-class content, citation styling, mono for code/data; restrained colour; occasional interactive diagram.
- **Says:** rigour, openness, ideas over branding.
- **Fits:** labs, research institutes, technical essays, think tanks. **Misfits:** consumer sales, hype launches.
- **Trap:** university template with logo crest and news carousel. Best escape by being the paper: long-form essays with interactive figures (Distill, Ink & Switch).
- **Load:** fast (medium with interactive figures).
- **Seen at:** inkandswitch.com — long-form research essays with figures; distill.pub — interactive explanatory ML papers; arcinstitute.org — clean institute site, science imagery.
- **Search:** "research lab website"; "tufte css"; siteinspire "science"; "distill-style article template".

### AI lab
- **Grammar:** two poles. Warm editorial (Anthropic: custom serif + sans, ivory #faf9f5 ground, clay accent, hand-drawn illustration); stark research (OpenAI: custom grotesk OpenAI Sans, black/white, large photographic or abstract gradient imagery); expressive (Mistral: pixel logo, sunset gradients, orange). Research posts as primary content.
- **Says:** serious stewards of powerful technology; human, not sci-fi.
- **Fits:** model labs, AI research orgs, safety/policy. **Misfits:** consumer playful apps, sales-heavy SaaS.
- **Trap:** neural-net glow, brain icons, purple "AI gradient" and sparkles. Best escape with a warm non-tech palette, editorial typography, and treating papers/essays as the product.
- **Load:** fast–medium.
- **Seen at:** anthropic.com — ivory, serif, editorial research hub; openai.com — monochrome grotesk with soft imagery; mistral.ai — pixel M, sunset gradients, orange CTA.
- **Search:** webdesignhot.com design.md anthropic; creativereview "Mistral design"; godly.website AI; "AI company brand identity" 2025.

### Architecture studio
- **Grammar:** project index (list or grid), architectural photography and drawings, grotesk type small, black/white, map/typology filters, rigorous grid.
- **Says:** rigour, spatial thinking, built work.
- **Fits:** architecture firms, landscape, urbanism, engineering studios. **Misfits:** playful consumer.
- **Trap:** full-bleed slider with tagline. Best escape with project databases and typology filters (OMA), or brand-forward play (BIG).
- **Load:** medium–high.
- **Seen at:** big.dk — playful project grid; oma.com — dense index of projects; snohetta.com — clean narrative case studies.
- **Search:** siiimple.com architecture; siteinspire architecture; godly.website architecture; awwwards architecture.

### Automotive / EV launch
- **Grammar:** studio car renders or film on black/white, wide-tracked minimalist sans (Polestar: Polestar Unica-style; Rivian custom), configurator UI, spec numerals huge, scroll-scrubbed reveals.
- **Says:** engineered future, calm confidence, design object.
- **Fits:** EVs, mobility, premium hardware. **Misfits:** budget, community, content.
- **Trap:** car on dark gradient with blue glow and "The future is electric". Best escape with Scandinavian restraint (Polestar), landscape storytelling (Rivian), or an honest configurator as hero.
- **Load:** high (video, 3D configurators).
- **Seen at:** polestar.com — minimal Scandinavian grid; rivian.com — adventure landscapes and product film; lucidmotors.com — luxury interior photography.
- **Search:** awwwards automotive; godly.website car; "EV launch website"; fontsinuse.com automotive.

### Bakery / food shop
- **Grammar:** the product photographed close and often (crumb, crust, trays, hands), a short daily-changing menu with prices, opening hours and address in the first screen, an order or pre-order action in the header, one warm accent (crust ochre, flour cream, a deep shop colour). The type carries the voice: monospaced and wide-tracked (Tartine), heritage serif (Poilâne), large serif over product pattern (Bread Ahead).
- **Says:** made today, by people, nearby; worth a detour.
- **Fits:** bakeries, patisseries, delis, grocers, cafés that bake, food producers with a shop. **Misfits:** large chains selling convenience, where the warmth reads as fake.
- **Trap:** the generic bakery site: darkened full-bleed photo of bread or floury hands, "Welcome to…" in a script or serif, warm brown everything, a photo slider, a PDF menu, a cookie banner over the hero. Also: a JS loader that shows a blank screen before the bread. The best escape through **one strong voice**, either in type (Tartine's monospace) or in the founder and craft (Poilâne's film), and by putting *today* (what is baked, what is left, when orders close) above the photograph.
- **Load:** medium (photography and video). Fast is possible when type and illustration carry it.
- **Seen at:** https://tartinebakery.com (monospaced wide-tracked voice, ochre order button, darkened hero photo); https://www.poilane.com (heritage serif wordmark, founder-led baking film as hero, e-boutique); https://www.breadahead.com (large serif headline over a grid of the product itself); https://www.e5bakehouse.com (the generic default: template nav, photo slider, cookie banner over the hero).
- **Search:** "artisan bakery website" on Siteinspire, Land-book, Godly; "bakery branding" on Behance and BP&O (bpando.org); Fonts In Use "bakery"; "boulangerie site web"; "padaria artesanal" for Portuguese references.

### Beauty / skincare
- **Grammar:** two poles: apothecary restraint (Aesop: serif/sans from packaging, amber tones, slow transitions) vs social-native pink and gloss (Glossier, Rhode). Texture swatch macro shots, ingredient lists.
- **Says:** ritual, efficacy, taste.
- **Fits:** skincare, fragrance, cosmetics, wellness retail. **Misfits:** tech infra, kids.
- **Trap:** blush pink + marble + gold serif. Best escape with packaging-derived layout (Aesop) or a single signature colour and product texture (Glossier pink, Rhode's glazed minimal).
- **Load:** medium.
- **Seen at:** aesop.com — Work & Co build, packaging-led calm; glossier.com — millennial pink, product-on-skin; rhodeskin.com — glazed minimal, product close-ups.
- **Search:** footer.design aesop; "beauty website design" godly; land-book.com beauty; "skincare ecommerce" siteinspire.

### Creative agency showcase
- **Grammar:** WebGL scenes, custom cursors, smooth-scroll (Lenis/Locomotive), giant display type, case-study reels, page transitions, preloader; often dark ground.
- **Says:** we can build anything you imagine; craft as spectacle.
- **Fits:** digital agencies, studios, creative technologists. **Misfits:** content-heavy, accessibility-critical, low-bandwidth audiences.
- **Trap:** the Awwwards SOTD template: preloader, distorted image hover, lowercase "we craft digital experiences". Best escape with one signature technique tied to the studio's thinking and fast case-study access.
- **Load:** high.
- **Seen at:** activetheory.net — real-time 3D world as homepage; locomotive.ca — smooth-scroll pioneers, typographic case studies; resn.co.nz — playful interactive oddity.

### Crypto / web3
- **Grammar:** dark or vivid gradient grounds, 3D renders (chrome, glass, iridescent), mono labels, pill buttons, token tickers, "Launch app" top-right; newer wallets go bright, soft and cartoonish (Phantom purple ghost, Rainbow).
- **Says:** frontier tech; or (newer) crypto made friendly and consumer.
- **Fits:** wallets, protocols, exchanges, on-chain apps. **Misfits:** heritage, regulated institutions wanting distance, low-tech audiences.
- **Trap:** space background, neon, floating coin, "decentralised future". Best escape with real product UI and one mascot/material (Uniswap pink unicorn, Phantom's purple), or radical plainness.
- **Load:** medium–high (3D renders).
- **Seen at:** uniswap.org — pink, swap widget as hero; phantom.com — soft purple, friendly wallet illustrations; rainbow.me — playful gradients and consumer tone.
- **Search:** web3designs.io; godly.website crypto; awwwards crypto; "web3 landing page" land-book.

### Dark product precision
- **Grammar:** a near-black ground, a tight ink ladder, one restrained
  accent, product UI as hero, masks, blends and corner glows, a signature
  curve (Linear, Vercel, Warp; `recipes-motion.md`).
- **Says:** fast, serious, built by craftspeople.
- **Fits:** software tools and platforms. **Misfits:** warm or physical subjects. It is also the default every AI reaches for, so propose it only when the subject earns it.
- **Trap:** "near-black plus one acid accent" with no masks or blends:
  the flat version is a slop cluster.
- **Load:** fast to medium.
- **Seen at:** linear.app: near-black, product UI as hero, masked glows; raycast.com: dark product shots with restrained accent and corner glow; warp.dev: the survey's own reference, live.
- **Search:** Godly (godly.website, dominated by this cluster); saasframe.io; Land-book "dark" plus "SaaS"; awwwards.com/websites/dark/; Lapa Ninja "dark".

### Developer docs
- **Grammar:** three-pane layout (nav / content / code), mono for code (Berkeley Mono, JetBrains Mono, Geist Mono), language tabs, copy buttons, search (⌘K), dense hierarchy, subtle syntax colour.
- **Says:** precise, complete, respects your time.
- **Fits:** APIs, SDKs, platforms, dev tools. **Misfits:** consumer marketing, lifestyle.
- **Trap:** default Docusaurus/Mintlify theme. Best escape with interactive request/response panes (Stripe), opinionated typography and examples-first pages (Tailwind).
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** docs.stripe.com — side-by-side code, personalised keys; vercel.com/docs — Geist type, minimalist panes; tailwindcss.com/docs — example-first with live previews.
- **Search:** "best developer documentation"; documentation.page; godly.website docs; "docs site design" Mintlify showcase.

### DTC challenger brand playful

- **Says:** we know this category is ridiculous; we are in on it.
- **Fits:** food & drink, household goods, CPG disruptors. **Misfits:** medicine, finance, grief, B2B.
- **Trap:** "quirky" pastel blob + rounded sans + exclamation marks. Best escape by having an actual point of view in the copy, and a type system as opinionated as the voice.
- **Load:** medium.
- **Seen at:** liquiddeath.com — black/white heavy-metal caps, irreverent copy; oatly.com — cream ground, hand-drawn type, editorial food talk; graza.co — squeezy-bottle product as mascot, punchy green.

### Fashion e-commerce experimental
- **Grammar:** brutal or deliberately "wrong" UI (system fonts, Times, Arial at odd sizes), editorial-first homepages, product on stark white, unexpected interactions (Balenciaga's scroll oddities), glitch or OS metaphors; abrupt cuts not eases.
- **Says:** culture-literate, avant-garde, retail as content.
- **Fits:** streetwear, avant-garde labels, concept stores, drops. **Misfits:** heritage luxury, mainstream retail, anything needing reassurance.
- **Trap:** neo-brutalist template with thick borders and yellow. Real examples are restrained in palette and radical only in structure — escape by making one convention (scroll, grid, cart) behave strangely while keeping checkout sane.
- **Load:** medium.
- **Seen at:** ssense.com — editorial-led homepage, white-ground product, museum curation; balenciaga.com — stripped, system-font utilitarian commerce; jacquemus.com — sun-bleached campaign imagery, playful object focus.
- **Search:** godly.website ecommerce; hoverstat.es fashion; "experimental ecommerce" awwwards; brutalistwebsites.com fashion.

### Fintech trust
- **Grammar:** grotesk/neo-grotesk (Söhne, custom Sohne-likes, Inter Display, Arcadia at Mercury) at tight tracking; near-white or deep navy ground; one saturated accent or a slow mesh gradient (Stripe); precise 1px hairlines, product-UI screenshots rendered as crisp vector cards, tabular numerals; motion is slow, eased, data-like (counters, flowing lines).
- **Says:** competent, regulated, engineered — your money is handled by adults who also ship good software.
- **Fits:** payments, banking-as-a-service, spend management, treasury, B2B infra. **Misfits:** consumer play, lifestyle, anything that wants warmth or mess.
- **Trap:** purple-to-blue gradient blob, Inter, floating dashboard mock tilted in 3D, "trusted by" logo row. Best escape with a single owned material (Stripe's animated gradient + exact typographic rhythm; Mercury's dusk photography and serif-tinged warmth; Ramp's acid-lime against black) and real product numbers.
- **Load:** medium (WebGL gradient at Stripe pushes high).
- **Seen at:** stripe.com — animated mesh gradient, documentation-grade type; mercury.com — muted dusk imagery, calm dark/light rhythm, private-bank feel; ramp.com — lime/black, bold claims backed by savings figures.
- **Search:** saaslandingpage.com fintech; godly.website "finance"; land-book.com fintech; "stripe gradient" breakdown; lapa.ninja finance.

### Government / civic
- **Grammar:** one accessible sans (GDS Transport / Arial fallback, Public Sans at USWDS), black on white, strong blue links, crown/header bar, 16–19px body, task-first headings, no imagery, no motion; components from a published design system.
- **Says:** this is the official, accessible, unshowy way to get a thing done.
- **Fits:** public services, civic tech, utilities, internal tools, accessibility-first products. **Misfits:** brand storytelling, fashion, entertainment.
- **Trap:** trying to look "modern" with hero carousels. The best embrace boringness: one thing per page, plain English, tested components.
- **Load:** fast.
- **Seen at:** gov.uk — the reference task-first service design; design-system.service.gov.uk — published components and patterns; designsystem.digital.gov — USWDS, Public Sans.
- **Search:** "GOV.UK design system"; "USWDS"; "civic tech design system"; designsystemsrepo.com government.

### Luxury fashion house

- **Fits:** fashion houses, jewellery, perfume, high-end galleries. **Misfits:** anything that must explain itself, mass retail, SaaS.

- **Load:** high (autoplay film, large imagery).
- **Seen at:** celine.com — Hedi Slimane-era black/white, wordmark and campaign only; bottegaveneta.com — house green and product-as-sculpture; jilsander.com — austere grid, small type.
